03How it works

Four steps, in plain words.

01
Send

The list of feeds you pay for and the decisions or signals each one reaches.

02
Weigh

We measure what each feed adds to those decisions, alone and together.

03
Compare

We find where feeds overlap, so shared value is counted once.

04
Report

You receive a ranking your finance team and your PMs can both read.

04Why it is different

Dropping one feed at a time can cancel the wrong one. Tare weighs feeds together, so overlap is not mistaken for value.

It answers in the units your decisions use, not in the vendor’s own metrics.

05What we need

What we need from you

  • The feeds you subscribe to and where each one is used
  • A history of the decisions or signals they inform
  • The renewal dates you care about

What stays with you

  • The raw vendor data and your licences
  • Your models and how they combine inputs
  • Anything a vendor contract does not allow you to share
