01The problem

“Our AI summarised forty annual reports overnight. Which of those numbers actually appear in the filings?”

The question Scribe answers.

02 · What you receive

Claims you can trace to the sentence.

Every claim your AI makes from a document, each with a pointer to the passage it came from and a plain verdict: verified or rejected. A reviewer can check any line in seconds.

Annual report · item 7verified

Revenue grew 12% year on year.

“Total revenue increased 12% compared with the prior fiscal year, driven by higher volumes in the services segment.”

Annual report · item 7rejected

Management expects margins to expand next year.

“Management does not provide guidance on future margins.”

03How it works

Four steps, in plain words.

01
Send

The documents, or the AI output you want checked against them.

02
Extract

Each claim is pulled out with a pointer to the passage it rests on.

03
Check

Ordinary code confirms the passage says what the claim says.

04
Record

Verified claims are kept with their source; rejected ones never enter.

04Why it is different

The check is code, not a second model asked for its opinion.
