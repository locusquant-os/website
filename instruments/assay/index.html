03How it works

Four steps, in plain words.

01
Send

Your backtest and the record of what was tried, including the versions you dropped.

02
Check

We rebuild the result and confirm the record of trials is complete.

03
Score

We score the result against everything that was tried to reach it.

04
Report

You receive a short report written for an allocator, not for us.

04Why it is different

It counts the tries you did not show, not only the one you did.

It is independent of the team that built the strategy, so the answer carries weight in diligence.

05What we need

What we need from you

  • The return series of the strategy you want assayed
  • A record, or an honest count, of the variations tried
  • A paragraph on what the strategy does

What stays with you

  • Your code, signals and parameters
  • Your raw data; it never leaves your environment
  • Position-level detail, unless you choose to share it
