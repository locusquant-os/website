3What was tried

Every one of the 400 recorded variations, by the Sharpe ratio it achieved. The promoted version sits at the far edge of what chance alone would produce from this many tries.

4What it means for an allocator

  • Expect results nearer the restated figure than the reported one.
  • The edge is real but modest; size and fees should reflect that.
  • Ask for the next twelve months to be reported against this baseline.

5What we saw, and what we did not

We worked from the return series and the record of variations supplied. We did not see code, signals or positions, and none left the manager’s environment. The restated figure is only as complete as the record of what was tried.
