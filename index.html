    Does our data leave our environment?

    No. Raw data stays with you. We agree what is needed in the first conversation, and where possible the work runs inside your environment.

    How do we start?

    Request access and tell us what you are working on. A founder replies, and the first call settles scope. Nothing is priced on the site.

    What is built today, and what is roadmap?

    Assay and Tare are available to design partners, Scribe is in pilot, and seven more instruments are in build, previewed on the instruments page. Our own fund runs its research stage live, on paper, with every decision audited. The rest is on the roadmap, stated plainly.

    Does the AI trade on its own?

    It proposes. Rules set by people decide what is allowed, and the rules are enforced below the agent, not in its prompt.

    Can we see the maths?

    The ideas, with toy numbers, are on the maths page. The detail comes in the conversation.
