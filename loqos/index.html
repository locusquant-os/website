01Physics, not policy

Not policy an agent obeys, physics it cannot break.

Prompt-level guardrails are guidance a model can talk its way around. LoQOS moves them below the agent, built from proven, deliberately boring parts.

NixOS

The same system, byte for byte, every time it boots. Nothing drifts.

eBPF

Kernel-level egress

02Guardrails that guide

A guardrail should steer, not just stop.

Five promises LoQOS makes to the people who set the limits, and to the agents that work inside them.

  1. Limits bound outcomes, not every step.Agents are free in how they work, and held to where they may end up.
  2. Every denial says which rule applied, and how to re-plan.An agent that is stopped learns why, so it does not guess its way into the next wall.
  3. A safe, reduce-only path is always open.Whatever binds, there is always a way to make the book smaller and safer.
  4. Every guardrail is proven by trying to break it.A rule that has not been attacked is a hope, not a control.
  5. Its cost is measured, not guessed.You see what each guardrail costs in missed opportunity, and decide with that in view.
