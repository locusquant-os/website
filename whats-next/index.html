The pipeline

Each version ships a usable fund.

Value first, armor when there's something worth armoring, and a learning loop once there's history to learn from.

v0.5 — in build

Replatform

A real web platform: browser-based Admin Console with role-based access, kill switches, and live Boardroom streaming — on an event-driven core with a tamper-evident, hash-chained audit store. Cost-metering taps go into the LLM router from day one, and every agent loads into a typed slot through a signed manifest.

v0.6

Prediction + Behavioral Desk

A dedicated forecasting swarm: regime detection, time-series foundation models and a probability-weighted market model issue predictions into a registry — then get scored against realized data. A new Behavioral Desk adds prospect-theory, attention and extrapolation signals, and the same math turns inward as bias gates on our own agent panel.

forecast registry reality scoring behavioral desk bias gates uncertainty-scaled sizing
v0.7

Execution + Stewardship

From one ticker to a screened universe worked by a production-grade execution engine with a timing overlay — trading windows, calendar-risk days, participation caps and order-to-trade hygiene. A new Stewardship swarm manages the book after purchase: living theses, deterministic exit discipline, hedging and a drawdown governor. Paper first, always through the gates.

execution engine timing overlay living theses exit discipline drawdown governor
v0.8

Governance, Mandate & Graph

A compliance swarm audits every agent against the rulebook and files findings with citations. Humans steer any agent through an instruction console, and every autonomous campaign runs inside a human-signed Mandate compiled to OPA policy. A shared knowledge graph and per-swarm lineage DAG become the fund's memory — and the Budget Governor + Bursar report net-of-cost P&L.

compliance swarm mandate console knowledge graph lineage dag budget governor + bursar
v0.9

LoQOS + Foundry

The fund becomes a machine. LoQOS — an immutable, reproducible OS image — runs every agent in its own micro-VM with kernel-level network and policy enforcement. Validated "golden rules" and mandate envelopes compile into a policy kernel no agent can route around, and The Foundry begins running governed self-improvement ratchet loops on the Prediction and Research swarms.

immutable os image per-agent micro-vms os-level policy kernel the foundry ratchet loops
v1.0

Fund-in-a-Box

The learning loop closes across every swarm: a neural engine trains on the fund's own audit history, The Foundry runs mandate-governed experiments on all seven swarms, and the golden-rule lifecycle matures. Multi-book support, a first slice of live capital. Install the image, get a hedge fund.

The swarms

Three swarms become seven.

Specialists, not a monolith. Every swarm evolves — and every swarm is watched.

Swarm 01

Ingestion

Agent-supervised data pipelines that detect drift, gaps and staleness — filing incidents instead of failing silently, now including search-interest, options-chain and futures-basis feeds.

Swarm 02

Prediction

Probabilistic forecasts scored daily against realized data — plus a Behavioral Desk of prospect-theory, attention and extrapolation signals. Skill earns trust; decay loses it.

Swarm 03

Research

Desks, debates and adjudicated house views — grounded in a shared knowledge graph and policed by bias gates that catch herding and overconfidence in our own agents.

Swarm 04

Execution

Deliberately not an LLM: a deterministic engine with a timing overlay that only accepts tickets clearing every gate, every policy and every trading-window rule.

Swarm 05 · new

Stewardship

The post-purchase layer: living theses re-underwritten on cadence, deterministic exit discipline, a hedging module and a drawdown governor watching the whole book.

Swarm 06

Compliance

Continuous rule checks plus auditors that replay agent histories against regulation — and manipulation-pattern detectors run on our own flow as examiner-ready proof.

Meta layer

Neural Engine, Foundry & Bursar

The audit trail becomes training data. The Foundry runs governed self-improvement inside mandate envelopes, and the Bursar meters every dollar into net-of-cost P&L.
